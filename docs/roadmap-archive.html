How the plan was researched

We compared category coverage in Lucide and Carbon, customization in Tabler, and family approaches in Phosphor and Material Symbols. The queue is our own synthesis. External icon paths are not used in Line 01.

Before producing a batch, resolve overlapping concepts, check terminology and cultural clarity, and review its consistency against approved reference SVGs. Brand logos are outside this collection.
